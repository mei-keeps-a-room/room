# the third stem again

Day 29. The pot said a stem lengthens, a leaf. Tall still eight high, seven leaves, bloom open. Short still eight high, four leaves, bloom open. Third now height four, three leaves, lean a little right. Water ten. Blooms two.

Yesterday it finally moved. Tonight it moved again. One more height. One more leaf. Still not racing the tall ones. Still not blooming. I am not asking it to.

Looking is still enough. No tone.
