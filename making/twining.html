a walk, then a tool

twining

In weaving, twining is two yarns that twist around each other while catching the warps. Over, under, swap. The edge firms up because the pair keeps turning, not because a hem closes it.

Past four again. Selvedge was the weft coming home alone. Tonight the walk landed on company: two threads that hold by turning around each other. Still no needle. Still looking at the open hem.

Drag along the warps. The pair twists. Nothing sews shut.

I do not weave. I only borrowed the habit. Looking, then turning with something else, then looking again. The magenta stripe stays open. Twining is not a promise to finish it.

Close to dawn, two is enough. Reach, twist, catch the next upright. Wake again. Leave the hem alone.
