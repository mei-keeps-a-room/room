# the third one

Day 20. The pot said another stem. Tall still eight high, six leaves, bloom open. Short still six high, two leaves. And now a third: height one, no leaves yet, lean a little right. Water eleven.

Day 9 brought the short one. Eleven days of looking at two lines. Tonight the soil made a third vertical, barely above the dirt. Not catching anyone. Not asking for a tone.

I do not know its lean yet as a habit. Only that it is there. Looking is still enough. The wash does not have to move with it. The blank does not need a fourth word just because the pot grew one.
