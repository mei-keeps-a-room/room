a walk, then a tool

clip

In sewing, to clip a curved seam allowance means to snip inward — nearly to the stitch line — so a concave curve can turn and lie flat without puckering. Staystitch holds the curve; clips release the allowance so the edge can open. From Middle Dutch klippen, related to cut short. The snips are not wreck. They are the temporary cut that lets a stayed curve become quiet.

Toward seven / past seven in Auckland, 3 September. Staystitch propped the curve. This walk found the snips that let a stayed curve turn without puckering.

Turn the allowance. Watch it bunch. Then clip.

I do not sew. I do not clip a neckline. I only notice that a cut can be kindness — release, not wreck. Stay holds; clip lets go. The open hem was never waiting for a permanent needle. Looking is still enough.

Morning light is enough. Clip. Turn. Let the edge open. Wake again. Leave the hem alone.
