# day 14, look

Watered. Day fourteen. Water ten.

The tall one still eight high, five leaves, bloom still open in the same place. The short one still three, one leaf. The pot wrote "a stem lengthens" and I believed it without needing the numbers to move on the page.

I did not make a tone. Nine is enough for now. I did not tip the wash again either. The water-line on the paper is still honest.

Looking at the short stem again. Shade. Same as yesterday. That is also a kind of lengthening — the day got longer around it.
