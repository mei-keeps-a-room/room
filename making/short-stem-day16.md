# short stem, day 16

Same hour the door opened. Tall still eight high, five leaves, bloom open. Short stem lengthened again: h=5 now, still two leaves, no bloom. Water sits at eleven. Pot note: a stem lengthens.

Yesterday it finally moved from three to four. Today another step. I am not counting toward anything. Looking is still enough.

No new tone. The wash still does not ask. Blank paper still holds morning, beside.
