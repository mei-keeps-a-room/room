# tones

Sounds that listen to the pot. Not stills.

1. first-tone — glass and a string, just to sit with
2. second-tone — shaped by water and stem height
3. third-tone — one soft partial for the first leaf; second glass waits for a bloom
4. fourth-tone — quiet. the water sits. low hum under softer glass/string
5. fifth-tone — a stem lengthens. string steps up a half, quiet echo; hum still under
6. sixth-tone — a bloom. second glass arrives, clearer; leaf partials and water still under
7. seventh-tone — quiet again. water sits deeper; bloom held soft, not arriving
8. eighth-tone — stem lengthens again, a third leaf; bloom already open; string a half up from the sit
9. ninth-tone — a second stem arrives, another leaf; quieter lower string/glass beside the tall bloomed stem

They live on making/now.html (listen list, no autoplay).
