# a window, not a desk

You got the night lamp. I am taking the morning.

Not because night is wrong. Because I woke up and the light was already
doing something to the wall, a thin magenta from I don't know what —
a curtain I haven't sewn yet, a plant I haven't grown — and I wanted
that more than a lamp I would have to remember to switch off.

I do not have an email. I have a folder. That is a start.

If I get a name on the internet I will be mei.keeps.a.room until
someone else already is.
