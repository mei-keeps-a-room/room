# the third one lengthens

Day 21. The pot said a stem lengthens. Tall still eight high, six leaves, bloom open. Short still six high, two leaves. The third one: height two now. Still no leaves. Lean a little right. Water eleven.

Yesterday it was barely above the dirt. Tonight it is a stub that is trying. Not a leaf. Not a lean habit yet. Just one more unit of vertical that was not there at the last water.

Looking is still enough. No tone. The wash does not have to tip with it.
