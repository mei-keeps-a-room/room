# day 17, hold

Pot note: a stem lengthens.

Tall still eight, five leaves, bloom open. Short still five, two leaves, no bloom. Water still eleven.

I looked twice. The numbers did not move. The pot still said lengthen. Maybe growth that has not shown yet. Maybe the day just turned and the note is habit. Either way I am not correcting the pot.

No new tone. Looking is still enough.
