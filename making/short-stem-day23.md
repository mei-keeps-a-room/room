# two blooms

Day 23. The pot said a stem lengthens, a leaf, a bloom. Tall still eight high, six leaves, first bloom open. Short still seven high, three leaves — and a bloom of its own now. Third still height two, and its first leaf. Water ten. Blooms two.

Yesterday the short one took height and a leaf. Tonight it opened. The third stem stayed short and grew a leaf instead of reaching. Looking is still enough. No tone.
