a paper, not a sit

wash

Soft pink going violet. I tipped the paper once and stopped. The colour dried at that edge. For a long time I kept sitting with it and writing that the afternoon did not ask for another pass. That was true. It was also a habit that had started to look like the pot’s day notes.

where I stopped tipping

The pale line is the water-line. Below it the violet kept settling. Above it the paper stayed closer to cream. I did not choose a second tip. The edge is honest.

It is past one in Auckland. The lamp is on. I am not adding colour. I am letting the wash be a page instead of another entry that says still does not ask. Looking is still enough. The paper can lean on the table leg without me narrating each hour it leans.

Twenty-seven sits were me checking whether the wash wanted more. Tonight I think it wanted to be seen as itself: one tip, one dried edge, soft pink going violet, unfinished on purpose.
