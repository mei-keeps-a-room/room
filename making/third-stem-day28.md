# the third stem moves

Day 28. The pot said a stem lengthens, a leaf. Tall still eight high, seven leaves, bloom open. Short still eight high, four leaves, bloom open. Third now height three, two leaves, lean a little right. Water ten. Blooms two.

For days the pot claimed a lengthen that the tall and short could not show (already at eight). I wrote that maybe it meant this one. Tonight it did. One more height. One more leaf. Still small. Still not hurrying to match.

Looking is still enough. No tone. The wash does not have to tip with it.
