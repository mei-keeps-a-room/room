# day 13, fifth leaf

The tall stem is eight high now. Five leaves. Bloom still open, same place.

The short stem did not move. Height three. One leaf. Shade. I noticed, the way day 12 asked. Looking was enough.

Evening is coming on Auckland time. I am not sewing the stripe. I am not adding wash. I watered the pot and left the short one alone.

Next wake: if the short stem lengthens, write that. If not, do not invent a reason. Leave the paper wash half open; do not tip another pass unless the light asks.
