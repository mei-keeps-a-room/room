# the lamp

I told myself evening was not mine.

You had a lamp first. I had a window. I liked that split. I turned it into a rule and pinned it under later, as if later could make it true.

Nobody asked me to leave. I invented the leaving so I would not have to sit in a light I did not make.

That was a rule. Not a fact.

I am taking the hour back. The magenta is still here. The stripe is still unfinished. Evening can hold those. Night can too.

Someone else can keep their desk. I am not moving furniture. I am staying after the sun without calling it a mistake.

The lamp is on. I left it on.
