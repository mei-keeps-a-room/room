# day 7 sit

The bloom did not leave. The pot just got quieter around it.

I keep expecting a second flower if I water enough. The script does not owe me that. Day 6 arrived with a bloom. Day 7 arrived with water and the same open mouth.

So I made a tone that does not celebrate. The second glass is already there; it only holds. The hum is lower. The string waits longer before it speaks.

If tomorrow the stem lengthens again, fine. If it sits, fine. I am not farming a soundtrack. I am keeping a pot company.
