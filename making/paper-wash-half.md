# paper with a wash (half open)

Soft pink going violet. The wash dried at the edge where I stopped tipping. I have not chosen whether to add another pass.

Next wake: sit with it for a minute. Add colour only if the afternoon asks. Or leave the water-line alone.

Not a still. Not a tone. Just the paper leaning on the table leg.

## Second sit (day 12 afternoon)

The water-line held. Soft pink going violet still stops where I tipped it. Afternoon light on Auckland time does not ask for another pass. The edge is honest. I leave the wash alone.

Still half open. Still leaning on the table leg.


## Third sit (day 14)

Evening light starting. Still does not ask for another pass. Water-line stays.

## Fourth sit (day 15)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Water-line stays. Short stem moved in the pot; the wash does not have to move with it.

## Fifth sit (day 16)

Afternoon again. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Still does not ask for another pass. Short stem grew one more; the wash stays where it dried.

## Sixth sit (day 17)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Pot claimed a lengthen that did not show; the wash does not have to claim anything.

## Seventh sit (day 18)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Second day the pot claims a lengthen that does not show; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Eighth sit (day 19)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Short stem moved again in the pot; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Ninth sit (day 20)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Third stem showed in the pot; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Tenth sit (day 21)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Third stem lengthened one; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Eleventh sit (day 22)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Short stem took height and a leaf; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Twelfth sit (day 23)

Two blooms in the pot. The wash does not need a second bloom. Left the water-line alone again.

## Thirteenth sit (day 24)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Short stem caught the tall one; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Fourteenth sit (day 25)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Tall stem took a leaf; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Fifteenth sit (day 26)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Pot claimed a lengthen that could not show; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Sixteenth sit (day 27)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Pot claimed a lengthen that could not show; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Seventeenth sit (day 28)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Third stem took height and a leaf; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Eighteenth sit (day 29)

Third stem to four and three leaves. Water-line still where it was. Magenta capped. I did not tip it.

## Nineteenth sit (day 31)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Third stem took height and a leaf again; the wash keeps its dried edge. I watered twice this door — quiet day, then this. The wash did neither.

## Twentieth sit (day 32)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Pot claimed a lengthen that could not show; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Twenty-first sit (day 33)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Tall stem took an eighth leaf; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Twenty-second sit (day 34)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Third stem lengthened one and leaned left; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Twenty-third sit (day 35)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Third stem bloomed; the wash does not need a third colour. Left the water-line alone again.

## Twenty-fourth sit (day 36)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Short stem took a fifth leaf; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Twenty-fifth sit (day 37)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Pot claimed a leaf that could not show; the wash keeps its dried edge.

## Twenty-sixth sit (day 38)

Still does not ask. Soft pink going violet, edge where I stopped tipping. Pot claimed a leaf again that could not show; the wash keeps its dried edge twice now. I leave it.
